A backend developer who spent years with Java and Spring. Lately I work with
Kotlin and event-driven architecture.

## What goes on this site

I write down what broke while building, and **the number I measured at the
time**. What went wrong and why tends to stay useful longer than a finished
result does.

Where I can, each post carries four things — what broke, why it broke, how I
fixed it, and the number I measured.
